# BharatLegal AI — Product direction

## Positioning
India-first legal AI workspace for advocates and law firms, expanding later to corporate legal teams and other professional users. Build original product identity and implementation; use leading legal-AI products as category benchmarks, not as material to copy.

## Initial wedge
Start with advocates and small/mid-sized law firms. The first paid workflow should solve a frequent, measurable task: understanding matter documents and producing a sourced research/drafting starting point.

## Product principles
- Primary-source grounding and traceable citations
- Explicit uncertainty; no fabricated authorities
- Confidentiality and security designed in, not added later
- Advocate remains responsible for legal judgment and filing
- Indian procedural and jurisdictional context
- Accessible pricing, validated with users

## Near-term validation
Interview 10 advocates across litigation and transactional practice. Observe current workflows, collect anonymised sample tasks, quantify time spent, identify must-have integrations, and test a paid pilot offer. Do not use real client-confidential documents without permission and suitable safeguards.

## Metrics
- Citation correctness on a curated benchmark
- Task completion time vs current workflow
- Weekly active professional users
- Repeat usage by matter/workflow
- Paid pilot conversion and retention
- Document-processing failure rate
- User-reported harmful or misleading outputs
