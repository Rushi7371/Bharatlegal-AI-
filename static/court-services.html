Search by case text, litigant / party, advocate or judge. Results depend on provider coverage and account permissions.

eCourts advanced searches

Choose the court location first, then enter the search detail. Year, district and court filters help narrow results. You only need the name or number relevant to the selected search.

Select State → District → Court Complex. Court / courtroom is optional. If you do not know the advocate or case number, choose Party name when you know a petitioner or respondent. If you have neither, use Case lookup with a CNR or the general Case Search tab with another known detail. Bar registration number and FIR-number searches are not currently supported by the connected partner API; they are not simulated. Results depend on provider coverage and permissions.

Judgment & precedent search

Search is powered by the configured eCourtsIndia partner index, not an official government judgment-search API. Search may use provider credits.

National Judicial Data Grid

View official national pendency and disposal dashboards inside the BharatLegal workspace. Dashboard figures remain sourced and controlled by the e-Committee / NIC.

Open official dashboard
NJDG data is supplied by the official eCourts dashboard. If the government portal blocks embedded viewing in this browser, use “Open official dashboard”.

BharatLegal AI does not scrape, alter or represent official NJDG figures as its own. The embedded dashboard is served by the Government's eCourts infrastructure.

My cases

Your local portfolio of CNRs, hearing notes and case details. Stored in this browser only; not yet synced to a server account.

Data integration: eCourtsIndia partner API, an independent third-party data provider, not a Government of India API. The official eCourts portal remains the authoritative source. Live case search, case detail, cause lists and order intelligence are subject to API permissions, provider coverage and usage charges. e-Filing, ePay, Virtual Courts, SMS/OTP, NSTEP and authenticated government actions are not represented as integrated here; those require their own official onboarding, credentials and transaction/security approvals. Never enter court portal passwords, OTPs or payment credentials into BharatLegal AI.
